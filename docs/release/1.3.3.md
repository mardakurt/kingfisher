application's Phase 87 changes; both are built from the same source.

Apple Silicon (arm64), **macOS 13 (Ventura) or later**. Signed with a
Developer ID certificate and notarised by Apple, ticket stapled to the disk
image and the application.

## What changed since 1.3.2

The full user-facing list is the 1.3.3 entry in
[`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md). The ones a Mac user will notice first:

- **The notation stays beside the board on a laptop-sized window**, and the
  board is larger there (556px instead of 452 at 1280x720).
- **The explorer's table fits its panel** — results as a small bar beside
  Score in the side panel, every column in a wide one — and the panel's
  title is the opening's name.
- **Layout → Engine under the board** shows the notation, the explorer and
  the engine's lines at once.
- **The sidebar starts with the research loop**: Analysis, Library,
  Databases, Openings, Players, Similar games.
- **Preparation** no longer calls a famous player gameless while the packs
  load, says what a pack records and what it keeps, and its dossier shows
  the opponent's score and results by first move and opening family.
- **The Library opens a game at the move you were previewing.**
- **Fixed:** the Start and End buttons showed each other's symbol; the
  Repertoire side panel could hide a position's moves on a short window.

Nothing under `desktop/` or `companion/` changed; the web application the
shell serves is what changed. The browser database stays at schema 23.

## Installing and updating

Download `Kingfisher-1.3.3-arm64.dmg`, open it, drag Kingfisher to
Applications, and double-click. An installed 1.1.8–1.3.2 is offered 1.3.3
through Sparkle's own window (_Kingfisher → Check for Updates…_); an
installed 1.1.0–1.1.7 is offered it by the engine those versions shipped
with. The work in `~/Library/Application Support/kingfisher-desktop/` is not
touched by the update.

## Verification

`npm run desktop:public:verify -- --landing --full` downloads and checks
them. The commands run for this release and their results are in
