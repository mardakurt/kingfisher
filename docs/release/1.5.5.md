# Kingfisher 1.5.5 — entering, replaying and reading games

**Type your moves.** Press `T` and type a move the way you would write it:
`Nf3`, `exd5`, `e8=Q`, `0-0`, `e2e4`, even `♘f3`. Paste a whole line from a
book or a chat — `1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 d6 3.d4` — and it is played as far as it is
legal, with what stopped it left in the field. Kingfisher never guesses:
when two pieces could make the move you typed, it says which two.

**Paste a game anywhere.** Copy a PGN or a FEN, press ⌘V on the page, and
Import opens with it already there.

**Read the clock.** A game with clock times shows both clocks at the move on
the board, and how long that move took. Turn on the evaluation graph and a
_Time per move_ strip sits under it, column for column, so a long think lines
up with the swing that followed.

**Win, draw, loss.** Stockfish 18 and Lc0 model the outcome as well as the
score. Kingfisher now asks for it, and the engine panel shows it: +0.2 in a
quiet Italian is a 94% draw by Stockfish's own estimate. An engine that does
not report one shows none — nothing is converted from centipawns.

**Watch a game.** `P` plays the line through, a move every 1.2 seconds.

**Send a position.** _Copy link to this line_ gives an address that opens the
position, and the moves that reached it, in anyone's Kingfisher.

**Import only what you want.** A minimum rating for both players or either, a
player, a span of years: _Keep only some games_ in Import PGN.

**Move sounds**, off unless you turn them on (Settings → Board).

**Security updates.** Next.js 16.4.1 includes the upstream fixes for two
critical remote-code-execution advisories and a high-severity denial of
service advisory ([upstream release](https://github.com/vercel/next.js/releases/tag/v16.4.1)).
The Mac shell uses Electron 44.5.0, and the test tools are updated too.

## Fixed

- PGN files exported on Windows keep their accented names: "Müller" was
  stored as "M�ller". Both the browser and the companion now read a file in
  its own encoding.
- Long algebraic PGN (`1. e2-e4`) imported with no moves at all; figurines,
  typographic dashes in `O–O`, lowercase piece letters and `½-½` each cut a
  game short. All read now; a move no single legal move answers is still
  refused.
- Spaced repetition counted a day as 24 hours, so a card reviewed late in
  the evening was not ready the next morning. Cards are due at the start of
  their day (04:00 local time).
- The promotion picker could not be used from the keyboard.
- A FEN refused for an impossible castling right is offered back without it,
  as an explicit choice.
- On a phone, the controls under the board fit one row; a study's name is no
  longer cut short in the header; a page with nothing on its board shows no
  other game's moves.
