## What changed since 1.3.1

The full user-facing list is the 1.3.2 entry in
[`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md). The ones a Mac user will notice first:

- **Daily works as a session**: a grade reschedules the card (it did not for
  repertoire cards), a reload continues where you were, and a finished day
  says so.
- **The Score Sheet no longer loses the moves typed before a reload**, and
  has a Take back button.
- **Opening Files link chapters and model games**, keep positions with the
  moves that reach them, and save notes as you type.
- **Endgame says which tablebase answers** the position on the board, and
  that lichess.org's online one receives the position.
- **Similar Games has a board** and results that open at the matching move.
- **Queries with "any of" and "not"**, saved and re-run; a **repertoire
  inbox**; **one list of analysis jobs**; a **ChessBase loss report**.
- **Fonts**: notation is in its monospaced face again (it was drawn in the
  system face since the Mac redesign), and caption text meets WCAG AA
  contrast in light and dark.
- **The companion** gains an optional compact position index, about
  eighteen times smaller on the position side and answering exactly as the
  original, and finishes an import that was interrupted on its next start.
  A common word searched in a ten-million-game database answers in under a
  second.

The shell under `desktop/` is unchanged; the companion and the web
application it serves are what changed.

## Installing and updating

Download `Kingfisher-1.3.2-arm64.dmg`, open it, drag Kingfisher to
Applications, and double-click. An installed 1.1.8–1.3.1 is offered 1.3.2
through Sparkle's own window (_Kingfisher → Check for Updates…_); an
installed 1.1.0–1.1.7 is offered it by the engine those versions shipped
with. The work in `~/Library/Application Support/kingfisher-desktop/` is not
touched by the update. The browser database moves forward to schema 23 on
first launch (saved queries, inbox decisions, write-back batches); the
migration is forward-only and every earlier schema has a fixture.
