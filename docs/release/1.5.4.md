# Kingfisher 1.5.4 — everything in a browser, and on every screen

**Your games, organised, with nothing installed.** The Databases page makes
collections beside My games, kept in your browser: import PGN into one,
search it and open its games in the Library, explore it as a source of its
own, copy, move and merge games between collections, rename and delete it.
A backup that includes games carries every collection. Switch on _Sync My
games and collections on this device_ in Account and they travel to your
other devices too: names and the newest 2,000 games, added and never
removed.

**Bring the database you have.** _Import En Croissant_ reads the `.db3`
file you choose, in the browser (up to 1 GB), into a new collection. _Import
ChessBase_ can put a database in a collection of its own. Import PGN takes a
file of any size, read a few megabytes of whole games at a time.

**Every screen.** On a phone, page names fit the header, Settings is one
column, the board sits centred, text fields no longer make iPhone Safari
zoom, and buttons are large enough for a finger. On an iPad the header gives
the page name priority. Installed on an iPhone, nothing sits under the notch
or the home bar. The public pages no longer scroll sideways on a 320 px
phone.

**Safari keeps your work.** Safari deletes a site's data after seven days
without a visit unless the site is installed. Kingfisher says so once, when
there is work to lose, with the three ways to keep it.

**Puzzles** are White or Black by a coin toss, then a random puzzle near
your rating.

What still needs the Mac application's companion: native engines other than
Stockfish 18 (which runs in the browser), Syzygy tables on your own disk (the
browser asks the Lichess tablebase), and En Croissant files over 1 GB.
