# Kingfisher 1.4.9 — one arrow, and the classics' faces

**On the board**

- Every board arrow has the engine best-move arrow's shape: the arrows you
  draw, the Explorer's, the engine's, and the arrows in an exported diagram.
- A plain right-drag draws a blue arrow that is the engine's best-move arrow —
  the same colour, halo, edge and opacity — on the board and in exported
  diagrams. ⌥ now draws green; ⇧ red and ⇧⌥ yellow are unchanged. Arrows
  already in your studies keep the colour they were drawn in, and every colour
  still round-trips through PGN `[%cal]`.

**Players**

- Historical players show their photograph, as titled players already did:
  Capablanca, Morphy, Steinitz, Lasker, Alekhine, Menchik and the rest of the
  pre-FIDE roster. FIDE titles begin in 1950, so the titled roster could never
  find them. Each person's Wikidata item is resolved from Wikidata itself and
  committed (`src/reference/legend-wikidata.json`); the picture is fetched
  from Wikimedia Commons only with its author and licence shown, and not at
  all with _Player photos_ off in Settings.

No new accounts, data formats or permissions. Backups are unchanged.
