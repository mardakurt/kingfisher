# Kingfisher 1.5.3 — accounts, sync, and research that remembers

**Optional accounts.** Sign up with email and password or with Google, on
kingfisherchess.app and in the Mac app. Signed in, your studies, chapters,
repertoires, training, preparation, opening files, journal, saved searches,
pinned lines, stored engine results, puzzle history, analysed positions and
settings follow you to every device you sign in on. When the same thing
changed in two places, the account's version is used and yours is kept for
review, never discarded. The first sign-in on a device that already has work
asks before uploading any of it. Your games can travel too, if you switch
_Sync My games on this device_ on: the newest 2,000, added and never removed.
Without an account nothing changes.

**New research tools.**

- _Opening map_: the next three moves of a local source as nested blocks,
  width for how often each was played and shade for the score; in
  Preparation, the same map from an opponent's own games.
- _Openings to reconsider_ (Review → Improvement): where your usual move
  keeps losing in your own games, and what scored better in a named source,
  with every count.
- _Compare with another position_ (Features): the squares that differ and
  the structural facts on each side.
- _Analysis history_: every position you analysed to depth 12 or more, on
  Recent, one click from the board.
- _Training progress_: eight weeks of reviews and what falls due next.
- _Describe_ a Library search in a sentence, train your costly moves, and
  edit a game's details on the board.

**A Studio that reads faster.** The tab strip appears only with a second
tab; a 1024×720 window shows the board beside the notation, engine and
explorer; engine lines use the whole row with the candidate move in bold; a
move number never ends a row without its move; the notation takes only the
room its moves need; an evaluation bar with no reading is blank; and empty
pages say what to do next.

**Fixed by using it.** A pass through every page at 1024×768 and on a
phone found nine layout faults. Each is fixed, and each now has a browser
test that fails without its fix: a chapter that scrolled the board away,
Review's header overflowing, an unreachable Notation control, clipped menus,
Puzzles' prompt below the fold, "W" for "White to play" on a phone, text
against panel edges, and empty states pointing "left" at lists that were not
there. The privacy, terms, security and data-licence pages now describe the
optional account.

**What is published where.** Live on kingfisherchess.app, and for the Mac
as `Kingfisher-1.5.3-arm64.dmg` (build 1228, commit `9d9065d6`): Developer
ID signed, notarised by Apple and stapled, and offered to installed copies
through the update feed.

Validation: unit, typecheck, lint, format, documentation and build gates;
the full browser suite in Chrome, Firefox and WebKit; the packaged Mac
certification (eleven gates) against this notarised build. The record is in
[the overhaul report](../product/2026-10-15-overhaul-report.md).
