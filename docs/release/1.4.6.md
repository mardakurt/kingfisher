# Kingfisher 1.4.6 — databases at hand, players named, openings by family

2026-10-04. [Comparison and boundaries](../product/chessbase-mac-comparison-1.4.6.md) ·
[Big data and accounts — a proposal](../product/big-data-and-accounts-proposal.md).

- **Databases in the sidebar.** Each database is listed under Databases with
  its game count, one click from any board, with **+** for a new one. An open
  database has its own address: Back returns to the grid, and a reload or a
  workspace tab keeps it open. The section you are on stays in view when the
  sidebar does not fit the window.
- **A database's contents, like ChessBase's database page.** Besides players
  and tournaments: its **openings** by family with White's score, its **top
  games** and **newest tournaments**, and its **annotators, sources and
  teams**, read from the games' own headers. Every row opens exactly its
  games in the Library — a tournament opens that edition, not every event
  sharing its words.
- **A game names its players.** Above the notation of an opened game, and in
  the Library's preview: both players with rating, photograph and country,
  the result, and event, round, site and date.
- **Country flags** beside player names in the Library, from the titled-player
  roster's country of citizenship (Wikidata). Unknown names get none.
- **Opening families in Preparation.** As White and As Black, each family
  with its wins, draws and losses, games and score; a family opens exactly
  its games. **Recent** opponents are offered on the Preparation start screen.
- **Back to where you were.** A game opened from the Library or a preparation
  report offers "Back to the Library: “…”" or "Back to preparation against …".
- **Frequent players in companion databases**, per Explorer move, under the
  same filters as the counts — for positions with up to 100,000 games, and a
  note past that.
- **Figurine notation** (Settings → Board → Piece notation): moves drawn with
  your piece set's figures in the notation and the move tables. PGN keeps
  letters, and a copied move is still "Nf3".
- The engine panel says what the engine is running with ("1 thread · 64 MB").
- **Fixed:** the Dossier left out an opponent's games under another spelling
  of their name; Set up position hid its FEN at laptop heights; Puzzles made
  Skip the most prominent control on an unsolved puzzle; "1 games".
- **Quoted search values are whole values**: `"Club Open 1"`,
  `"Queen's Gambit"` and `"Capablanca"` match exactly that event, opening
  family or tag.
- **Release:** `SHA256SUMS` now reads with `shasum -a 256 -c`.

No new persistence store or schema migration; one new preference, which
defaults to letters. Accounts are not added — see the proposal for why and
for the alternative.
