# Kingfisher 1.4.2 — puzzles, tournament tables, threats, diagrams and engine matches

Release notes, 2026-10-03. Seven features ChessBase advertises and Kingfisher
had no version of, each built from data Kingfisher may redistribute and each
labelled with what it is computed from. The comparison behind the list is
[`chessbase-comparison-2026-10-03.md`](../product/chessbase-comparison-2026-10-03.md).

- **Puzzles.** 24,587 rated tactics puzzles from the Lichess puzzle database
  (CC0), chosen reproducibly from 6,157,341 and replayed through Kingfisher's
  rules. Pick a theme and a difficulty; your solver rating is Glicko-2 over your
  own attempts, kept in the profile and in backups. Works offline. A hint or a
  wrong move records the attempt as unsolved; any checkmate is accepted.
- **Tournament tables.** From any game in the Library, the event's cross-table
  — round-robin grid or swiss standings — built from the games the database
  holds, with Sonneborn-Berger, Buchholz and FIDE performance. Progress by
  round; every result opens its game; copy as text. Missing pairings make it
  provisional, never complete.
- **Threats and safety.** The Features panel lists pieces the other side wins
  material by capturing and pieces nobody defends, and can ask the engine what
  the other side threatens (the position with the turn passed), naming the
  engine, depth and time.
- **Diagrams.** Copy the board as an image, or save it as PNG or SVG, with your
  piece set, board colours, coordinates and the arrows you drew.
- **Engine matches.** Two engines, or two sessions of one, play from the board's
  position with colours alternating. The score comes with an Elo difference and
  its 95% interval, the likelihood of superiority and the number of adjudicated
  games. Save the games as a study or copy the PGN.
- **Rating history.** The player page plots the rating each game recorded, per
  month, and the FIDE performance per year.
- **Guess the Move on any game**, in Analysis and Studies.

Schema version 24 adds one portable store, `puzzleAttempts`; a 1.4.1 profile
upgrades in place and its backups restore into 1.4.2.

Not in this release: ChessBase's proprietary corpora and services, cloud sync,
voice input, or any measured comparison with ChessBase itself. See the
comparison document for why.
