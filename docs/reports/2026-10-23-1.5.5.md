## Found and fixed

- **The promotion picker could not be used from the keyboard.** It chose on
  pointerdown only, and lost its own focus: a click-click move commits on
  pointerdown and the browser's mousedown then moved focus to the page.
- **Long algebraic PGN imported with no moves** (`1. e2-e4` read as a move
  to e2), and figurines, `O–O` with a typographic dash, `nf3` and `½-½`
  each cut a game short.
- **Windows-1252 PGN lost every accented name** ("Müller" → "M�ller"), in the
  browser and in the companion: both decoded every file as UTF-8, silently.
- **Spaced repetition counted a day as 24 hours**, so a card reviewed late
  was not due the next morning; "1 months"; the Daily endgame pick turned
  over at UTC midnight.
- **A study's name was cut to "Sicilian …" at 1440 px**: the header measured
  its room before the name arrived and never again.
- **Studies with no chapter showed another game's moves** in its notation.
- **Shortcut hints disagreed with the bindings**, including a wrong hint for
  moving a variation up. The palette, notation menu and board controls now
  read the person's live binding; initial server labels remain hydration-safe.
- **The packager pinned an older Electron independently of the dependency.**
  Updating the dependency alone would still have shipped 44.2.0. A new
  package/configuration consistency check failed against that old pin; both
  now name 44.5.0, and the downloaded binary reports that version too.
- **On a phone the controls under the board clipped** once Play and Type were
  added; now 32 px steps on one row, wrapping at 320 px.
- **A recorded "open defect" that never existed**: reference packs were said
  to file a `*` game as a Black win; the scan has rejected every undecided
  game since the builder was written. The design note and the ledger now say
  so. A stale ledger row ("shortcut remapping missing") was removed — it has
  existed since Phase 10.

## Added

Typed moves and whole lines (`T`); paste a PGN or FEN anywhere to import;
both clocks at the move on the board; time per move under the evaluation
graph; the engine's own win/draw/loss (`UCI_ShowWDL`); play a line through
(`P`); a link that opens a line (`?moves=`); import only the games wanted
(rating, player, years); optional move sounds made with Web Audio; a refused
FEN offered back without impossible castling rights; copy an engine line as
text; the line's link from a move's own menu.
