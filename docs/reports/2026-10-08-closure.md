## Puzzle behaviour

- Correct moves show a check and positive feedback, and wait for the opponent's
  reply before enabling another move.
- Incorrect moves show a cross and retry feedback, then restore the same
  decision. The expected answer is not disclosed.
- A wrong move or hint records one unsolved rated attempt. Subsequent correct
  moves can complete the puzzle in practice without changing that result.
- Revealing the solution offers an interactive practice retry.
- The wrong-move regression fails against the previous implementation.
