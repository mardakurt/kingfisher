## Server boundaries and costs

The [existing proposal](big-data-and-accounts-proposal.md) remains the design.
Accounts need identity/recovery, per-record revisions, offline conflict handling,
scoped sharing/revocation, backups, export/delete and revised privacy claims.
Cloud databases then store the portable records. A community evaluation pool
adds position+engine+depth identity, bounded uploads, abuse handling and moderation.
Estimate: several weeks of development/security review plus ongoing service
ownership; hosting budget requires workload limits.
Design budget, not an implementation result: accounts/recovery/revocation and
revision-conflict sync 3–6 engineer-weeks; scoped cloud database storage/export
2–4 additional weeks; community evaluation uploads, identity, quotas and abuse
handling 3–6 additional weeks. These are estimates, with ongoing operational
ownership, not delivery commitments.

A small managed deployment could start with [Supabase Pro](https://supabase.com/pricing)
at $25/month plus usage/compute outside its allowances (price checked
2026-10-06). Blob storage can use [R2 Standard](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/)
at $0.015/GB-month plus request charges and no egress charge: 100 GB is $1.50
before free allowances, by arithmetic. These are infrastructure examples,
not a full budget: email, monitoring, backups, moderation, staff time and
capacity growth remain to be costed. Engine rental/128-core compute is a
separate paid service. Start with explicit per-user storage/request limits,
spend alerts and an export/delete path; choose retention and recovery targets
before approving service work.

No paid infrastructure or account service is approved by this request. No server
is provisioned. Lichess cloud evaluation and portable backups remain available.
