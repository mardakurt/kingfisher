Engines

From a browser Stockfish to a Leela workstation.

Stockfish 18 is in the browser, sandboxed and instant. Native engines install through Settings, with their digest verified and their licence on the row. Two engines can run side by side.

  • Stockfish 18WebAssembly (browser) · native (desktop)
  • Berserk 14native, digest-verified
  • Halogen 16.0.0native, digest-verified
  • Koivisto 9.0native, digest-verified
  • Obsidian 16.0native, digest-verified
  • PlentyChess 8.0native, digest-verified
  • Stormphrax 8native, digest-verified
  • Viridithas 20native, digest-verified
  • Lc0 0.32.1system Leela, qualification on macOS

Engines run on your machine. Their digests are checked before they run. The interface says which one is running and why.

Local-first

Your chess stays on your machine.

No account is required. No data is sent back. Stockfish answers without leaving the browser. Reference data installs once and works offline. The diagnostic report is local; the log file is local; your studies and repertoire are local.

No telemetry

The product does not phone home. There is nothing in the network panel during a normal session except the engines and reference data you asked for.

No hidden upload

Studies, repertoire, training and notes are stored locally. Restoring from backup does not require contacting a server.

Open source

Every line that runs on your machine is in the repository. Releases, digests and the changelog are public.
