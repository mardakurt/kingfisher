Opening research

Compare sources. Never merge them silently.

Most chess tools show one "truth" score that quietly blends every population of games. Kingfisher shows what each source says — and lets you read them against each other rather than pretending they say the same thing.

Databases

Search what you already have.

Attach large personal collections. Run position, player, structure and claim searches against them. Open positions and games back into the board.

Position search

Material, piece placement, pawn structure, king position.

Player search

12,522 identities plus historical figures. Diacritics and aliases.

Structure search

Find the positions in your collection that share a shape.

En Croissant import

Bring collections from En Croissant and other PGN/SQLite tools.

Backup & restore

Round-trip your authored work without round-tripping the reference data.

Copy, move, merge

Combine and dedupe collections. Federated search across them.

Engines

From a browser Stockfish to a Leela workstation.

Stockfish 18 is in the browser, sandboxed and instant. On macOS, native engines install through Settings with their digest verified and their licence on the row. Two engines can run side by side.

Local-first

Your chess stays on your machine.

No account is required. No data is sent back. Stockfish answers without leaving the browser. Reference data installs once and works offline. The diagnostic report is local; the log file is local; your studies and repertoire are local.

No account

Open Kingfisher, use it. There is no sign-up, no subscription, no email capture.

No telemetry

There is nothing in the network panel during a normal session except the engines and reference data you asked for.

Open source

Every line that runs on your machine is in the repository. MIT-licensed, public, transparent.
